Pulled pork is one of the easiest ways to use a slow cooker. A pork shoulder has plenty of fat and connective tissue, which is exactly what long, gentle cooking turns soft and juicy. You rub the meat, set it on a bed of onion, and let the slow cooker do the work.

This version keeps the flavors simple: a sweet and smoky rub, a little vinegar for brightness, and the pork's own juices to keep everything moist. It makes enough for a crowd and the leftovers freeze well, so it suits weekend cooking as well as weeknight meals.

Cooking times vary between slow cookers, so go by texture and temperature rather than the clock. The pork is ready when it shreds without a fight.
