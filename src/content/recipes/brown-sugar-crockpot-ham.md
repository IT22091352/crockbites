  - title: "Step 1 – Fit the ham in the slow cooker"
    text: "Discard the plastic packaging, bone guard, and any commercial seasoning packet. Place the ham cut-side down into an oval 6-quart or 7-quart slow cooker. If the lid does not close fully, seal the top with a double layer of heavy-duty aluminum foil before placing the lid on top."

### Why This Recipe Works
Ovens often dry out spiral hams because the cut slices allow moisture to evaporate into the dry baking chamber. In the sealed, moist environment of a slow cooker, the ham gently warms in its own juices while absorbing the spiced brown sugar and pineapple glaze, keeping every single slice luscious and tender.
