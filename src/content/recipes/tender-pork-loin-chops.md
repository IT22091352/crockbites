### Why This Recipe Works
Pork loin is notoriously lean, meaning high heat quickly dries it out. By cooking 1-inch thick chops on Low in a moist, acidic braise of apple cider and Dijon mustard, the muscle fibers gently relax without seizing. Finishing with a quick slurry turns the savory cooking juices into a rich pan sauce.
