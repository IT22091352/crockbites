instructions:
  - title: Brown the beef
    text: In a skillet over medium-high heat, cook the ground beef and chopped onions until the beef is browned (reaching at least 160°F / 71°C) and the onions are tender. Drain excess fat.
  - title: Combine in slow cooker
    text: Transfer the browned beef and onions to the slow cooker. Add the minced garlic, drained kidney beans, crushed tomatoes, chili powder, ground cumin, and salt.
  - title: Mix and cook
    text: Stir all the ingredients together until well combined. Cover and cook on LOW for 6 to 8 hours, or on HIGH for 3 to 4 hours.
  - title: Garnish and serve
    text: Stir well before serving. Ladle into bowls and serve hot with your favorite toppings.
foodSafety:

  - Refrigerate leftover chili in shallow airtight containers within 2 hours of cooking, and reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C).
