instructions:
  - title: Step 1 – Brown the beef
    text: In a skillet over medium-high heat, cook the ground beef and chopped onions until the beef is browned and onions are tender. Drain excess fat.
  - title: Step 2 – Combine in slow cooker
    text: Transfer the browned beef and onions to the slow cooker. Add the minced garlic, kidney beans, crushed tomatoes, chili powder, cumin, and salt.
  - title: Step 3 – Mix and cook
    text: Stir all the ingredients together until well combined. Cover and cook on Low for 6 to 8 hours, or on High for 3 to 4 hours.
  - title: Step 4 – Garnish and serve
    text: Stir well before serving. Ladle into bowls and serve hot with your favorite toppings.
