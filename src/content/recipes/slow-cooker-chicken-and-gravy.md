Chicken and gravy is simple comfort food. The chicken cooks gently with onion, garlic and herbs until tender, and the juices become the base of a savory gravy you thicken right in the slow cooker.

Many versions rely on canned cream soup. This one builds the gravy from broth, a little Worcestershire sauce and cornstarch, so you control the seasoning and the thickness. Cream is optional if you want it richer.

Serve it over something that can soak up the gravy, such as mashed potatoes, noodles or rice.
