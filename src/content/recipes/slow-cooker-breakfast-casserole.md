A slow cooker breakfast casserole is a practical brunch dish. You layer hash browns, sausage and cheese, pour over an egg mixture, and the casserole cooks gently while you do other things.

The key with eggs in a slow cooker is moderation. They can turn rubbery or brown at the edges if cooked too long, so check the casserole early and rely on a thermometer to confirm it is cooked through.

Prep the sausage and vegetables the day before if you like, and keep the pieces separate in the refrigerator until you are ready to assemble.
