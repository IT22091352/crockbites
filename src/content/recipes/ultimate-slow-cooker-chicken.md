instructions:
  - title: "Step 1 – Build the vegetable base"
    text: "Place the thick onion slices and smashed garlic cloves across the bottom of the slow cooker. This props the chicken up so it roasts rather than boils in its juices."
  - title: "Step 2 – Season the bird"
    text: "Pat the chicken completely dry with paper towels. Rub olive oil all over the skin. In a small bowl, mix the paprika, garlic powder, thyme, oregano, salt, and pepper, then rub the spice mixture evenly across the entire chicken."
  - title: "Step 3 – Set in the cooker"
    text: "Pour the chicken broth gently around the base of the cooker without washing the spice rub off the top. Place the chicken breast-side up on top of the onions."
  - title: "Step 4 – Cook to temperature"
    text: "Cover and cook on Low for 5 to 6 hours (or High for 3 to 4 hours). Check that the thickest part of the thigh registers at least 165°F (74°C) on an instant-read meat thermometer."
  - title: "Step 5 – Rest and crisp"
    text: "Carefully transfer the chicken to a rimmed baking sheet. For crispy skin, pop under your oven broiler on high for 3 to 5 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before carving."

### Why This Recipe Works
Slow cooking a whole chicken traps moisture so the breast meat stays tender instead of drying out. The onion trivet keeps the bottom from stewing in rendered liquid, while a quick 3-minute oven broil at the end gives you the golden, crackling skin of a traditional roasted chicken with none of the weekday stress.
