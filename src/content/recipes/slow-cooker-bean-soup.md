Bean soup is inexpensive, filling and well suited to a slow cooker. White beans turn creamy as they cook, and carrots, celery, onion and garlic build a savory base. A smoked ham hock adds depth, but it is optional.

Dried beans need a little planning: they are soaked first, then boiled briefly before they go into the slow cooker. That short boil matters for food safety, and it also gives you more predictable cooking.

The soup freezes well, so it is a good one to make in a big batch.
