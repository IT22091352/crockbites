### Why This Recipe Works
Creamy chicken recipes in a slow cooker often curdle if dairy is cooked from the start. By simmering the chicken and sun-dried tomatoes in seasoned broth first, then stirring in heavy cream, freshly grated Parmesan, and cornstarch in the final half-hour, you get an ultra-silky, rich sauce with chicken that is juicy and fork-tender.
