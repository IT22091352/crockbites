Beef stew is what slow cookers were made for. Chuck roast has plenty of connective tissue, and hours of gentle heat turn it silky and tender while the potatoes and carrots soak up the gravy.

This recipe keeps the ingredients familiar: beef, potatoes, carrots, celery and a savory broth with tomato paste and thyme. Browning the beef first adds a deeper flavor, but it is optional if you want a true set-and-forget meal.

Peas stirred in at the end add a bright color and a fresh bite after the long simmer.
