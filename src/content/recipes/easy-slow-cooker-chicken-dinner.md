This is the dinner you assemble in the morning and walk away from. Chicken thighs, potatoes, carrots and onion cook together in one pot, and the chicken's juices flavor the vegetables underneath.

Bone-in thighs are forgiving and stay tender during a long cook. A simple butter and herb seasoning is all it needs, and the cooking juices make a light sauce for the whole plate.

There is only one pot to wash, which suits busy weeknights.
